The concept

A simple idea: turn spare hours into real money.

Students have flexible time but few ways to earn safely. Local shops have short bursts of work but no easy way to find reliable help. GetBoth brings the two together in one trusted place.

🎓

For students

Campus life is expensive. Traditional part-time jobs demand fixed schedules, long commutes and prior experience — impossible when classes come first.

🏪

For local businesses

A café needs an extra pair of hands for the morning rush. A shop needs someone for two hours on Saturday. Posting a full-time contract is overkill.

🤝

GetBoth matches them

Businesses post a short, clearly-priced shift. Students browse offers near them, see the rate, duration and city upfront, then accept instantly. No CV, no interview, no waiting weeks for a reply.

One platform. Real shifts. Fair pay. ⭐

How students earn money

Small shifts. Real money. Paid on time.

Every offer shows the exact hourly rate and duration before you accept — so you always know what you'll earn. Money is credited to your GetBoth balance only when the business confirms your work is finished.

How it works

From browsing to paid in four steps.

Trust & control

Fair for students. Useful for businesses.

The whole lifecycle is transparent on both sides — no ambiguity about who is doing what, or when money is earned.

📊

Status you can see

Offers move PENDING → ACCEPTED → ENDED, and every student's work shows Accepted, Finished or Cancelled.

🧾

Business-managed payouts

Only the business marks work finished — that action credits pay, minutes worked and the shift count together.

⭐

Reputation that travels

Businesses rate students 1–5 stars with an optional note. Reviews appear on student dashboards and applicant lists.

🛡️

Moderated community

Admins suspend or ban with a written reason. Businesses can cancel work or remove an offer — with clear statuses for everyone.

⏱️

Privacy built in

Chats expire automatically 24 hours after a shift is accepted and are deleted from the database with all messages.
